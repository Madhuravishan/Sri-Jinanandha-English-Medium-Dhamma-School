Our Heritage & History

A legacy of virtue, wisdom, and compassionate teaching.

Marking the pride of half a century of noble Dhamma service, our Dhamma School began as a humble endeavor and stands unwavering today, having awakened the wisdom of thousands of children.

No matter how much society changes, our dedication to gifting the nation a virtuous, respectful, and moral generation through the pure teachings of the Buddha remains our highest priority. We guide children on the right path by cultivating loving-kindness (Metta) and patience in their hearts.

Supported by the Venerable Maha Sangha and our dedicated volunteer teachers, we continue to illuminate the spiritual path for our youth.

Our Mission & Core Values

To impart the teachings of Buddhism in English, fostering discipline, mindfulness, and empathy in our youth to navigate modern challenges.

💡

Wisdom (Paññā)

Cultivating deep understanding of the Dhamma through structured English-medium education.

❤️

Compassion (Mettā)

Teaching children to approach all living beings with boundless loving-kindness.

🌱

Virtue (Sīla)

Building strong moral character based on the timeless precepts of the Buddha.

🧘

Mindfulness (Sati)

Practicing daily meditation to develop focus, calmness, and mental clarity.

Guiding Principles: The Five Noble Precepts

  • I undertake the precept to refrain from destroying living creatures. (Cultivating Compassion)
  • I undertake the precept to refrain from taking that which is not given. (Cultivating Generosity)
  • I undertake the precept to refrain from sexual misconduct. (Cultivating Contentment)
  • I undertake the precept to refrain from incorrect speech. (Cultivating Truthfulness)
  • I undertake the precept to refrain from intoxicating drinks and drugs which lead to carelessness. (Cultivating Mindfulness)
